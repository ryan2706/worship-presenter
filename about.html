About Worship Presenter

A free app for home groups. It puts song words and Bible verses on a TV, so everyone can sing and read together.

Open it on a laptop connected to the TV. Pick songs and verses in this window, and they show up big and clear in the TV window. You can also plan the order of songs and readings for a meeting, and go through them with one button.

Your songs stay on this device

Songs and services you add are saved only in this web browser, on this device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts.

If this browser's data is cleared, they are gone. Use Back up my songs to save a file you can keep, and Restore from backup to bring them back on this or another device.

Songs and copyright

The sample hymns were all published before 1929, so they are in the public domain. For songs still under copyright, your group needs a CCLI licence.

Bible text

Both Bibles come from eBible.org and are in the public domain.

  • King James Version (KJV), 1769 standard text. eBible.org says: “You may copy the King James Version of the Holy Bible freely.” The text is provided courtesy of the CrossWire Bible Society and eBible.org. In the United Kingdom, Crown letters patent cover printing the KJV, not showing it on a screen.
  • World English Bible (WEB), the WEB Updated edition with “the LORD”. It is in the public domain. “World English Bible” is a trademark of eBible.org, so the name is only used for faithful copies.

Every verse is kept word for word. Footnotes, headings, Psalm titles and the Apocrypha are left out.
