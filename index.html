How to put it on the TV

  1. Press Open display. A new window opens.
  2. Drag that window onto the TV.
  3. Click inside it and press Full screen, or the F key.
  4. Come back here, pick a song, then pick a slide.

If the window won't move onto the TV, open your computer's display settings and choose Extend these displays instead of Mirror or Duplicate.
